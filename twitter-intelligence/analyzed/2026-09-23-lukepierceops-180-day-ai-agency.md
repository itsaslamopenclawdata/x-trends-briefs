Disclaimer: results not guaranteed; numbers from “my agency and the operators I work with”; not financial advice.

**Thesis:** $500k/yr is a *schedule*, not a vibe. Floor offer for $2M–$10M companies:

- Paid audit **$3,000** (credited to build)
- Build **$30,000** (Boom claimed real closes $25–60k)
- Retainer **$3,000/mo** (Boom claimed $3–8k)

**Why “advisor retainers” fail without a brand:** $2k/mo for two meetings/month only sells if the seller already has an audience. $40k/mo names “walk into the room before they do.” Unbranded operators need an offer that sells on **its own math**.

**Funnel (author’s “call it” rates):** 12 builds/yr ÷ 60% audit→build = 20 audits; ÷ 40% discovery→audit = **50 discovery calls/yr ≈ 1 qualified call/week**.

**Phases:**
1. D1–14: one industry, $2–10M ICP, hook = one pain + one outcome (never lead with “audit”), lock $3k/$30k/$3k, one outbound channel (LI or email + warmup), one case study.
2. D15–45: 20–30 min/day outbound (LI 30 connects/day or email 20–30 sends/day; breakup email “outperforms”); 1 post/day; discovery → book strategy call before hang-up; strategy sells audit or the one narrow solution — never $33k on call 1.
3. D46–90: 2-week audits (inventory, bottlenecks, ROI-ranked automations, phased budget); first $30k build by d75–90. Revenue still small; asset is the weekly-call pipeline.
4. D91–135: 8–10 week delivery with **daily Slack demo items**; do not pause outbound; reuse components in-niche (“second build 80% done”); optional workshop.
5. D136–180: first retainer ~d150; 4–5 clients signed; 1 new build/month.

**Year-1 spreadsheet (floor, 12 new clients):** 12×$3k audits = $36k; 12×$30k builds = $360k; 45 retainer-months × $3k = $135k; **total $531k**. Year-end 9 retainers = $27k/mo before new sales.

**Channel math (UNVERIFIED rates):** LI 7,800 req/yr × 20% accept × 3% of connections on a call ≈ 47 calls; email 5,000 × 1% ≈ 50; content + workshops → **70–80 calls vs 50 needed**.

**Six break points:** quit month 2; lead with audit in outreach; propose too much too early; sell to firms that can’t pay (<$500k ARR); stop outreach during delivery; end calls without next booked.

**CTA:** Google-doc 180-day checklist + **Inner Circle** (DM “INNER CIRCLE”; paid; not free). Follow-ups: 80+ members, 8 live calls/week, Slack, onboarding 5 this week; author claimed “hundreds of DMs, ~75% about Inner Circle” after this article took off.
