**Phase 1 — Foundation (~6 weeks):** agent-readiness audit; identify the 3–5 workflows agents should drive; define guardrails/gates pattern.
**Phase 2 — MCP Service (~12 weeks):** expose core workflows as MCP tools; gated harness so agents act safely; eval suite proving reliability.

**Outcome:** your product is operable by AI agents; your own team ships through the gated harness first (trust starts in-house).

**Moat:** delivery playbooks + gate-design IP + vertical specialization.
